1. Who we are

Data controller: [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], address: [ADDRESS], contact email: [EMAIL]. Talent Foxes helps parents motivate children to move and tidy up: a child completes tasks, a parent approves them, and the child earns stars and grows a virtual pet.

2. Who the app is for

The app is managed by a parent or legal guardian. A parent signs in with a Google account and creates the child's profile. A child cannot register alone. By creating a child profile, the parent confirms being the child's parent or legal guardian and consents to the processing of the child's data under this policy.

4. Legal bases

  • Performance of the agreement with the parent (the Terms) to run the service.
  • Consent of the parent or legal guardian to process the child's data. It can be withdrawn at any time by deleting the child profile or the family.
  • Legitimate interest in security and fixing faults.

5. Who receives the data

Data is stored and processed by our infrastructure provider, Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore in the European region eur3, and Firebase Cloud Messaging when notifications are enabled). Google acts as our processor. Firebase Authentication may process data outside the EU; the transfer is covered by the EU standard contractual clauses.

Spotify and Yandex Music playlist links open in external apps. We do not pass the child's data to them; the parent chooses the link.

6. How long we keep data

As long as the family or profile exists. When you delete a child profile or the whole family in the app (Settings, "Data and deletion"), the data is removed from the database immediately. Copies in the provider's backup systems disappear within the period the provider sets. Children's sign-in records may remain in the authentication service as technical records without data until they are removed in routine clean-up, on request, or with the child's password at deletion.

7. Your rights

You may access, correct, delete, restrict, receive a copy of your data and object to processing. A parent can do most of this in the app. For the rest write to [EMAIL]; we reply within one month. You may also complain to a supervisory authority: the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy) or the authority in your country.

8. Security

Data travels over secure connections. Only the parents of a family can access that family's data, while a child sees only their own profile and the family's shared rules. Database access rules are enforced by the server.

9. Children

We show children no ads, do not profile them, and do not ask for more data than the app needs. A parent can view the child's data in the app at any time, change it or delete the profile.

10. Changes

If the policy changes materially we will tell you in the app. The current version is always at this address.
